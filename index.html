A music workspace built with JavaScript and Web Audio API

Where Music Theory Meets Algorithmic Composition

ShenoBin brings music learning and creative production together. Compose melodies, explore chords, and build drum patterns you can hear right in your browser.

Open Workspace 🎹

Tools for making and learning music

From sketching the first notes to shaping an arrangement and exporting your work.

Melody Generator

Enter a few notes and the Markov-based engine follows your melodic patterns to suggest new continuations.

Built-in Synthesizer

Shape your sound with ADSR envelopes, several oscillator waveforms, and built-in delay and reverb effects.

Drum Pattern Generator

Choose a style such as rock, trap, or techno, generate a rhythm, and adjust its dynamics and feel.

Interactive Music Theory

Explore relationships between keys with the Circle of Fifths and see matching notes in the piano roll.

Song Structure Wizard

Arrange sections into a song outline and use genre templates and starting points to develop an arrangement.

Harmony Tools

Calculate and invert chords, then explore harmonic directions with suggestions in the selected key.
