About ShenoBin

ShenoBin is a browser-based music composition and music theory workspace. It brings a piano roll, sound tools, theory visualizations, and idea-generation features together so musicians can sketch, hear, and refine musical ideas in one place.

What it helps with

ShenoBin is designed to make music theory easier to explore while reducing the friction between a musical idea and a playable sketch. Learners and educators can visualize keys, scales, chords, and the Circle of Fifths; songwriters and producers can draft patterns, hear them with synthesized instruments, and continue developing them in a desktop music application.

Tools in the workspace

  • Piano-roll composition and playback with tempo, key, and scale controls.
  • Chord calculation and recommendations, plus an interactive Circle of Fifths.
  • Melody continuation based on Markov-chain transitions and drum-pattern generation.
  • Built-in synthesizer sounds and practice-mode chord display.
  • Local project files and export to MIDI or WAV.

Who it is for

ShenoBin is useful for music students, teachers demonstrating harmonic relationships, songwriters developing arrangements, and producers who want a quick browser-based sketchpad. It runs as a web app in a modern browser; the repository also includes a Chrome extension package. The workspace needs JavaScript and Web Audio support. It is not a replacement for a full desktop DAW.
